# Not Boring Media — Bundle Plan

Your 99 single passages are already live at $2.99 each. **Bundling them is the
single biggest revenue lever you have right now** — it raises how much each
buyer spends per order, and bundles rank well in TPT search. Creating them costs
nothing and reuses products you've already uploaded.

## Why bundles make money

- A teacher who would buy *one* $2.99 passage will often buy a $19.99 themed set
  instead — higher revenue per customer.
- Bundles show a big "you save X%" number, which TPT shoppers love.
- A "Growing Bundle" (the Mega Bundle below) lets you charge once and add future
  passages for free, which drives early purchases and locks in loyal buyers.

## How to create a bundle on TPT (5 minutes each)

Bundles are **not** uploaded as files. You assemble them from products you've
already published:

1. Seller dashboard → **Add New Product** → choose **Bundle** (not Digital
   Download).
2. Give it the title and description from `products/bundles.csv`.
3. **Add existing products** — search your store and select the passages listed
   for that bundle (the `included_filenames` column tells you which numbers).
4. Set the bundle price (TPT will confirm it's below the combined total).
5. Set grades 4–8 and the same subject/tag categories you use on the singles.
6. Save as **draft**, preview it, then publish.

Start with **Animals & Wild Creatures** and the **Mega Bundle** — animals are the
strongest seller for upper-elementary, and the mega bundle captures your biggest
spenders.
